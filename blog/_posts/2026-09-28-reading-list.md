### Background

I am creating a reading list of online resources that are useful. There are many great physical books, but this list is for online, digital copies. I am creating this so that I can pull these up and read them when I have time.  

In all honesty, this is the beginning of a massive AI policy outline. 


### Papers
1. "[Attention is All You Need](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_Is_All_You_Need)"
2. "[AI as Normal Technology](https://knightcolumbia.org/content/ai-as-normal-technology)"
3. "[NVIDIA Tesla: A Unified Graphics and Computing Architecture](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/afs/cs/academic/class/15869-f11/www/readings/lindholm08_tesla.pdf)"


### AI Misue
1. [Google Threat Intelligence Group: The Evolution of Adversarial AI](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/from-prompting-to-autonomy-the-evolution-of-adversarial-ai) 
2. [Detecting and Countering Misuse of AI: September 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026) 

### AI Safety
1. [Key Problems in AI Infrastructure Security](https://multipliercg.substack.com/p/key-problems-in-ai-infrastructure)
2. [Concrete Problems in AI Safety, Revisted](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.10899)
3. [Securing AI Model Weights](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA2849-1.html)


### AI in China
1. [China's AI Ecosystem: A Background Explainer](https://oxfordchinapolicylab.org/research/china-s-ai-ecosystem-a-background-explainer)

### Books
1. [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) by Chip Huyen
2. [Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback](https://rlhfbook.com/) by Nathan Lambert
3. [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio and Aaron Courville

Don't buy the AI Engineering book, but instead check for it at your local library.
