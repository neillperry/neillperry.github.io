### Background

I am creating a reading list of online resources that are useful. There are many great physical books, but this list is for online, digital copies. I am creating this so that I can pull these up and read them when I have time.  

In all honesty, this is the beginning of a massive AI policy outline. 

### Foundationals
1. "[A Logical Calculus of Ideas immanent in Nervous Acvitity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/bf02459570)" by McCulloch and Pitts
2. "[Prediction and Entropy of Printed English](https://www.princeton.edu/~wbialek/rome/refs/shannon_51.pdf)" by C.E. Shannon
3. "[The Perceptron: A Perceiving and Recognizing Automaton](https://web.archive.org/web/20230407120421/https://blogs.umass.edu/brain-wars/files/2016/03/rosenblatt-1957.pdf)" by Rosenblatt
4. "[Perceptrons: An Introduction to Computational Geometry](https://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/3132/PerceptronsAn-Introduction-to-Computational)" by Minsky and Papert
5. "[Learning Representations by Back-Propagating Errors](https://www.nature.com/articles/323533a0)" by Rumelhart, Hinton, and Williams
6. "[ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2012/file/c399862d3b9d6b76c8436e924a68c45b-Paper.pdf)" by Krizhevsky, Sutskever, and Hinton
7. "[Attention is All You Need](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_Is_All_You_Need)" by a bunch of AI scientists who are all now rich

### Books
1. [AI Engineering](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/) by Chip Huyen
2. [Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback](https://rlhfbook.com/) by Nathan Lambert
3. [Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio and Aaron Courville
4. [Neural Networks and Deep Learning](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/neural-networks-and/9781492037354/) by Aurélien Géron

Don't buy the AI Engineering book, but instead check for it at your local library.
